# Teacher-first editorial review

Reviewed 20 September 2026. This records an independent read of the actual authored teaching, followed by a second read of all 64 final extension cards after the authors corrected repetition. It is not inferred from file existence, schema validity or word counts.

The review read every beat's explanation, visual states and relationships, handbook text, linked knowledge checks and answer rationales, plus each module's recap and applied task. The sixteen modules contain 135 beats. Reused canonical checks were assessed by meaning, since they retain their original concept IDs rather than being renamed to match new teaching beats. The 43 canonical lessons, 144 existing cards and 96 existing checks remain separately preserved.

The final extension review compared prompts, answers and complete explanations with the module's complete main teaching, not just its title. The initial draft contained substantial repetition. Forty overlapping extensions were replaced: eighteen in the technical group and twenty-two in the field/GenAI/architecture group. Retained adjacent cards were read again, and the latter group's retained explanations were expanded. Shared principles across modules are applied to distinct mechanisms—for example, revising a decision record, measuring corrective-action efficacy and recording a rolled-back delivery are different tasks. The exact author dispositions are in [curriculum-1-8.md](curriculum-1-8.md) and [EXTENSION-AUDIT-9-16.json](EXTENSION-AUDIT-9-16.json).

Three concrete teaching/rendering defects were found and corrected during the review:

- The transformation after-state omitted the cross-partition contributions South=5 and North=8. Both edges now appear, and the text alternative includes the same relationships and totals.
- The architecture rollback arrow pointed toward the new path. It now returns consumers from the new path to the retained old path, including in the text alternative.
- The capstone link nested inline-concept notation inside Markdown link text, which displayed the notation literally. It now uses a plain, correctly linked capstone label.

The review also prompted source-verified extension replacements for task exclusion, task values, batch consumption, grouped filtering and approximate percentiles. Their product evidence was read directly in the official [dependency conditions](https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/jobs/run-if), [task values](https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/jobs/task-values), [foreachBatch](https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/structured-streaming/foreach), [HAVING](https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/sql/language-manual/sql-ref-syntax-qry-select-having) and [approx_percentile](https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/sql/language-manual/functions/approx_percentile) documentation. Worked quantities and design judgments are original teaching examples, not quoted vendor examples or executed product results.

Current product details receiving an additional independent source check on the final revision included deletion-vector logical removal versus physical rewrite, current automatic/legacy change-feed distinctions, partial federation pushdown and JDBC fetch size, Snowflake scaling-policy applicability, BigLake delegated access and Free Edition use limitations. These were checked against the publisher's [deletion-vector documentation](https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/tables/features/deletion-vectors), [change-feed documentation](https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/tables/features/change-data-feed), [federation performance guide](https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/query-federation/performance-recommendations), [multi-cluster warehouse guide](https://docs.snowflake.com/en/user-guide/warehouses-multicluster), [BigLake introduction](https://docs.cloud.google.com/bigquery/docs/biglake-intro) and [Free Edition limitations](https://docs.databricks.com/aws/en/getting-started/free-edition-limitations). Other source records retain their authors' explicit passage, date and context evidence; this document does not claim every external page was independently reread a second time.

No further substantive teaching defect was found in the reviewed final text. This is an editorial finding, not a guarantee of perfect content or proof that a learner has mastered it. Authored numeric examples were checked by reasoning and arithmetic. The preserved exercise bundle has separate execution evidence. This review did not execute a Databricks workspace, cloud migration, commercial pilot, real model evaluation or fault injection.

`tests/teaching-content.test.ts` separately checks the actual release inventory and canonical identities, every beat's usable associations, all sixteen reviewed-media placements and four extensions per module. It rejects broken source/concept/anchor/card/media references, invalid visual relationships and answer sets, unsafe links, credential-bearing URLs, untrusted players and invalid segments. It also validates the unrelated photography course with zero, one and five extension cards and different beat counts, so release counts do not become generic engine constraints. These deterministic checks support the review; they do not replace semantic judgment or browser evidence.
