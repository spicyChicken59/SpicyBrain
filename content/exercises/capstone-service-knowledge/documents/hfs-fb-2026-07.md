## Scope

This bulletin applies to Ridgeback R2 units whose serial number begins with **R2C** (built from September 2025). It does not apply to R2A or R2B units, which continue to follow HFS-SM-R2-3.0 §5.1.

## Change

For R2C units, torque the four discharge valve retaining bolts in a cross pattern to **42 N·m** (synthetic value), not 38 N·m. The revised valve seat on R2C units needs the higher clamping load; the 38 N·m figure has produced weeping at the seat on two R2C units in the fictional incident log.

## Everything else unchanged

Gasket replacement at every removal, the cross pattern and the gasket kit part number are unchanged. If the serial prefix cannot be read, treat the unit as unidentified and ask the site contact for the asset record before torquing.

## Retirement

This bulletin will be folded into HFS-SM-R2 version 3.1 when that version becomes effective. Until then it is the authority for R2C valve torque.
