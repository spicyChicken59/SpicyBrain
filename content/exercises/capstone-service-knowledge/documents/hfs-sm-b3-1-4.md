## 3.1 Belt replacement

1. Confirm the isolation record for this ticket (HFS-ISO-014, current version).
2. Remove the guard and slacken the motor slide until the belt lifts free by hand.
3. Fit the replacement belt set (HFS-P-B3-0118) as a matched set; never mix a new belt with a used one on the same drive.
4. Tension with gauge HFS-TG-2 to 6 mm deflection at the marked load. Hand-tensioning by feel is not accepted.
5. Rotate the drive two turns by hand, re-check deflection, refit the guard.

## 3.2 Coastal sites

Drive brackets at coastal sites follow the interval in HFS-CST-022, which is issued only to endorsed technicians. Non-endorsed technicians raise a ticket for a coastal inspection rather than performing it.
