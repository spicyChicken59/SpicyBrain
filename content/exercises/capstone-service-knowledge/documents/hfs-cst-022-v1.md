## Restriction

This supplement is issued only to technicians who hold the coastal endorsement recorded in the training system. It is not to be displayed to, quoted to, or summarised for other technicians. Its existence may be acknowledged; its content may not be shown.

## 2.1 Inspection intervals (coastal sites)

| Asset | Standard interval | Coastal interval |
|---|---|---|
| Ridgeback R2 discharge housing | 12 months | 6 months |
| Aldercroft AP-7 casing bolts | 12 months | 4 months |
| Brindle B3 drive bracket | 24 months | 12 months |

Values are synthetic.

## 2.2 Reporting

Corrosion findings are recorded against the asset with the photo reference and the inspection date. A finding on a casing bolt at coastal interval triggers replacement, not re-torque.
