## 4.3 Drive-belt tension

1. Confirm the isolation record for this ticket exists (see HFS-ISO-014, current version).
2. Remove the belt guard (four captive fasteners).
3. Using the tension gauge HFS-TG-2, apply the gauge at the mid-span of the longest belt run.
4. Target deflection is 9 mm at the gauge's marked load. Adjust the tensioner bolt until the gauge reads within 8–10 mm.
5. Refit the guard before the isolation is released.

Hand-pressed "feel" tensioning is not an accepted method for the R2. Record the gauge reading in the ticket.

## 5.1 Discharge valve retaining bolts

Torque the four discharge valve retaining bolts in a cross pattern to **38 N·m** (synthetic value). Replace the valve gasket at every removal; do not re-use a compressed gasket.

Units with serial prefix **R2C** carry a revised valve seat. Check HFS-FB-2026-07 before torquing an R2C unit; the bulletin takes precedence over this section for those units only.

## 5.2 Gasket part reference

Valve gasket kit: part HFS-P-R2-0412. One kit per valve. Ordering follows HFS-PR-04.
