## Access note

Mechanical seal work on the AP-7 is restricted to level-2 technicians. Level-1 technicians may perform the external inspection in §3 and must raise a ticket for seal work.

## 6.2 Mechanical seal replacement

1. Confirm the isolation record for this ticket (HFS-ISO-014, current version) and that the suction and discharge valves are locked closed.
2. Drain the casing through the lower plug into the site's approved container.
3. Remove the coupling guard and the spacer coupling.
4. Withdraw the back pull-out assembly; support the shaft before the last bolt is released.
5. Remove the old seal. Inspect the shaft sleeve for scoring; a scored sleeve is replaced, not polished.
6. Fit the new seal (kit HFS-P-AP7-0207) dry. Do not lubricate the seal faces.
7. Reassemble, set the seal working length to the mark on the sleeve, refit the coupling and guard.
8. Release isolation per HFS-ISO-014 and run for ten minutes; a seal that drips more than one drop per minute after ten minutes is refitted, not "run in".

## 6.3 Coastal sites

Coastal-region sites use an additional inspection interval defined in HFS-CST-022. That supplement is issued only to technicians holding the coastal endorsement.
