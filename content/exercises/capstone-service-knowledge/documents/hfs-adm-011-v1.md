## Claiming mileage

Mileage for travel to a site in a personal vehicle is claimed weekly in the expenses portal against the ticket id. Attach the odometer readings. Claims older than 60 days are not accepted. Fleet vehicles do not attract mileage claims; their fuel is charged to the fleet cost centre.
