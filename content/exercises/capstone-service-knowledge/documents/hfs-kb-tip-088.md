## Tip

If you're in a hurry and the gauge isn't in the van, you can set B3 and R2 belts by hand: press the mid-span with your thumb, about a finger's width of give is fine. Works for me.

Also, if the previous shift already tagged the machine you don't need to redo the restart check, it's obviously isolated.
